About

Welcome to my research website. My work focuses on developing and evaluating intelligent systems with an emphasis on trustworthy, human-centered, and responsible artificial intelligence. I collaborate with undergraduate and graduate students on research projects across several areas of AI.

Research Areas

Large Language Models

Reliability, hallucination detection, evaluation, reasoning, and responsible use of LLMs.

Trustworthy AI

Safety, transparency, uncertainty, verification, and trustworthy AI systems.

AI in Education

Intelligent learning environments, AI-assisted feedback, assessment, and educational applications.

Human-Centered AI

Designing AI systems that consider human needs, behaviour, interaction, and decision-making.
