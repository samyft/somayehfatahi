About

Welcome to my research website. My work focuses on developing and evaluating intelligent systems with an emphasis on trustworthy, human-centered, and responsible artificial intelligence. I collaborate with undergraduate and graduate students on research projects across several areas of artificial intelligence.

Research Areas

Large Language Models

Reliability, hallucination detection, evaluation, reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation, and responsible use of LLMs.

Trustworthy AI

AI safety, transparency, uncertainty, verification, reliability, and responsible AI systems.

AI in Education

Intelligent learning environments, AI-assisted feedback, assessment, educational data mining, and learning analytics.

Human-Centered AI

Designing AI systems that consider human needs, behaviour, interaction, trust, and decision-making.

Health AI

Artificial intelligence applications in healthcare, safety-critical systems, and human-AI interaction.

Machine Learning

Machine learning methods, intelligent systems, classification, prediction, and applied AI.
